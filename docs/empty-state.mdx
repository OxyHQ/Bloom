# Empty state

A list with nothing in it still has to say something, and until this family existed every
list in Bloom said it differently. Twelve components had each drawn their own block: five
of them the same glyph-over-headline-over-line, at five paddings and four maximum widths,
under six spellings of the same two strings — `emptyTitle`/`emptyDescription`,
`emptyMessage`, `emptyQueueHint`, `emptyLabel`, `emptyText`, `noResultsLabel`.

```tsx
import { EmptyState } from '@oxy.so/bloom/empty-state';
import { RiInbox2Line } from '@oxy.so/bloom/icons/RiInbox2Line';

<EmptyState
  icon={RiInbox2Line}
  title="Nothing in this folder"
  description="Mail you file here will show up in this list."
  action={{ label: 'Write a message', onPress: compose }}
/>;
```

## The two rungs are about WHERE, not about how much there is to say

`comfortable` is the screen: a 32 glyph, a headline, 48 of air above and below, and the
text capped at 360. `compact` is the panel — a side panel, a popover, a card that is
already inside something: a 24 glyph, a body title, 24 of air, capped at 280. Nothing
else changes, so a list that moves from a screen into a panel changes a prop and not a
component.

The 360 is the number worth defending. The five hand-rolled blocks this family replaces
used 280, 260, 360, 340 and none, and the one with none is the reason: a centred line of
explanation that runs the full width of a 1440 page needs a second fixation per line, and
every author who noticed picked a different cap. 360 is roughly sixty characters at
`body-regular`.

## The mark: three ways, and when each is right

- **`icon` + `media="glyph"`** (the default) is the bare mark in the GRAPHICAL rung —
  `textGraphical`, the ladder stop sized for a line rather than for text, so it clears
  3:1 on whatever surface the block was dropped on. It is the quiet answer, right when
  the empty state is REPORTING (a filter matched nothing, a folder is empty).
- **`icon` + `media="circle"`** is `IconCircle`, the tinted disc. Louder; right when the
  block is the whole screen, or when it is INVITING rather than reporting.
- **`illustration`** is an arbitrary node and wins over `icon`, drawn exactly as it comes
  with no tile of its own. It is read for PRESENCE, so `illustration={null}` draws no
  mark at all even when an `icon` arrived from a default two levels up.

There is no fourth. A neutral "well" and a tinted disc side by side were two answers to
one question, and both shipped in this repo.

## The actions are DATA, and there are at most two

A node would have worked — the six blocks this replaces each passed one, which is how
three of them ended up with three different button sizes for the same button. As
`EmptyStateAction` the family owns the size (medium, or small at the panel rung), the
order and the wrap; the caller owns the words and the handler. Anything that is not a
button — a row of suggestion pills, a link, a note — goes in `footer`, under them.

`children` is the third slot, between the explanation and the actions. It is stretched to
the block's full width and left to lay itself out, because what goes there is a strip, a
fact box or a list rather than centred text. `order-confirmation` is built on exactly
that: a "done" screen is the same object as a "nothing here" screen with a different
reason for existing.

## It is ONE named group

The whole block carries `role="group"` named by the title and the description joined. A
screen reader that reaches an empty list should be told the situation; a heading, a
paragraph and a button with nothing joining them are three stops that each say a third of
it. The glyph is hidden from assistive technology — it repeats what the title says, and
an unnamed image between a heading and its paragraph is a stop that announces nothing.

## `minHeight` is for holding a band open

A table body, a panel that must not collapse. The content centres in it. Without it the
block is exactly as tall as what it contains, which is what a list wants.

## Migrating a hand-rolled one

Not done yet, and deliberately: this family is designed to cover the existing call sites
as they stand, and moving them is a separate change with its own screenshots. The ones it
was measured against are `cart-panel`'s `EmptyBasket`, `carrier-quote`'s empty offers
list, `mail-list`, `chat-list`, `address`'s `AddressList` and `data-table`'s empty band —
six shapes between them, from a 32-glyph column to a text-only 160-tall band.
