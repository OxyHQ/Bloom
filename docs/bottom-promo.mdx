# BottomPromo

A bottom-of-screen call to action: a fade into the surface, then a solid block
with a centred headline, an optional supporting line and a full-width primary
action. It is the invitation a signed-out reader sees at the foot of the main
panel.

```tsx
import { BottomPromo } from '@oxy.so/bloom';

<BottomPromo
  title="Don’t miss what’s happening"
  description="People on Mention are the first to know."
  actionLabel="Sign In"
  onAction={signIn}
/>;
```

`BottomPromo` renders content only. Place it in the bottom slot of a `Screen`,
`AppShell` or `PageFooter` so the surrounding layout owns its position and
reserves its measured height. The fade and the block take the surrounding
surface fill, and the block adds the bottom safe area on its own.

`title` and `description` are centred. `actionLabel` and `onAction` drive the
full-width inverse button. `style` styles the outer block and `testID` addresses it.
