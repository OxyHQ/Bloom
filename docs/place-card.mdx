# Place card

```tsx
import { PlaceCard, PlaceActions } from '@oxy.so/bloom/place-card';

<PlaceCard
  name="Forner de la Plaça"
  category="Bakery · €€"
  photo={photo}
  rating={4.6}
  reviewCount={318}
  openState="open"
  hours="Open until 20:00"
  address="Plaça de les Bruixes 4"
  facts={[{ icon: RiWalkLine, label: '6 min' }, { label: '450 m' }]}
  onPress={open}
/>
```

## Two densities, one component

`density="row"` is the result in a list over the map. `density="detail"` is the header of
the sheet that opens when the result is picked. They are ONE component because they carry
the same facts in the same order — a name, what the place is, how well it is thought of,
whether it is open, how far away it is and what you can do with it. A second component
would be a second place for "open until eight" to be spelled, and two spellings drift.

Only the arrangement differs:

| | row | detail |
| --- | --- | --- |
| photo | 112 square thumbnail, radius 12 | a 165 cover across the top |
| name | `body-semibold` | `title-2-medium` |
| rating | `Rating` at `small`, beside the name | `Rating` at `medium`, beside the name |
| figure | — | a quiet label over a tabular `title-1` reading |
| tiles | — | `stats`, one row from 640 wide, two columns below |
| actions | under the row | under the figure block |

## Nothing here is drawn twice

Every part of this card already existed, and this family composes them rather than
redrawing them:

- the stars are **`Rating`**, exactly as `ListingCard` uses it — beside the title, never
  pushing it out;
- the open/closed pill is **`Badge`** on a tone (`success` / `warning` / `error`), which is
  the library's one labelled status pill;
- the distance chip on the detail header is **`Chip`**;
- the address line and the facts row are **`listing-card`'s own parts**
  (`ListingLocationLine`, `ListingFacts`), imported, so a pin and a clipped fact row look
  the same in a place card and in a listing card because they are the same code;
- the row's 112 thumbnail is `listing-card`'s `COMPACT_PHOTO_SIZE` and
  `COMPACT_PHOTO_RADIUS`, not a second pair of numbers;
- the heart is **`FavoriteButton`**;
- the actions are Bloom **`Button`s** through `PlaceActions`;
- every colour is read off the surface the card was dropped on
  (`styles/surface-levels.ts`), so the card is right on the page, in a sheet and inside a
  `ContentPanel` without being told which.

The REVIEWS are not here either. [`place-reviews`](/docs/place-reviews) already draws the
summary, the review cards and the write-a-review prompt; a sheet renders
`PlaceReviewSummary` under this header rather than this header growing a second one.

## The detail header is `ai-profile-card`'s register

A cover band, a name at title weight, a quiet label over a figure, tiles, and labelled
`secondary` actions. That is deliberate: a place sheet and a profile card are the same
shape of object — a picture, a name, one number that matters and a row of things to do —
and Bloom should not have two registers for it.

The one thing it does NOT copy is the avatar. A profile has a face over the cover; a place
has only its photograph, so the cover IS the photo and the content starts under it.

## Open, closing soon, closed, opens soon

Four states rather than a boolean, because "closes in fifteen minutes" is a different
answer from "open" and an app that can only say `true` says the reassuring one. The word
comes from `PLACE_OPEN_LABELS` (or `openLabel`), the tone from `PLACE_OPEN_TONE`.

**The pill and the hours are separate lines.** The pill is a WORD ("Open") and the hours
are a SENTENCE ("Open until 20:00"). Pushed onto one line the sentence truncates first and
leaves the pill saying half of what the app knows.

## The row is a link and the controls are its siblings

The thumbnail and the text sit inside ONE pressable — a real `<a href>` on web when `href`
is set. The heart is laid over its corner and the actions sit under it, both from OUTSIDE
that pressable. A button inside an anchor is invalid HTML, and on web a click on the heart
would ALSO open the place.

The row is one press target, so it carries one composed name — `composePlaceName` puts the
name, the badge, the category, the rating as a sentence, the state, the hours, the address
and the facts in reading order. Without it a screen reader reads "Forner de la Plaça Bakery
€€ Open Open until twenty hundred" as one run, with the rating announced as "star four
point six paren three one eight".

## Known gap: an action has no pressed state

"Save" is a toggle in every maps app, and Bloom's `Button` declares no `aria-pressed` and no
`accessibilityState` — a component with a closed prop list drops what it does not name. So a
saved place is expressed by the action's own WORDS and glyph ("Save" → "Saved",
`RiBookmarkLine` → `RiBookmarkFill`), which is the one spelling that reaches both platforms
today.

That is a real shortfall and it is recorded rather than worked around: `PlaceActions` does
not hand-roll a second labelled button to get the attribute. The fix belongs on `Button`
(`pressed?: boolean`, emitting both spellings, exactly as `GlyphButton` already does).

The heart is not affected — `FavoriteButton` carries both spellings itself.

## Geometry

`PLACE_CARD_GEOMETRY` holds the detail side: the 165 cover, the 24 radius, 16 of padding,
15 between blocks, 10 for a tile's corner and padding, 10 between actions. The row side has
no geometry of its own on purpose — it is `listing-card`'s compact row, part for part.
