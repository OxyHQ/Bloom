The page around them — facts, floor plans, the agent or landlord card — is in
[`@oxy.so/bloom/listing-details`](./listing-details) (`PropertyFacts`, `FloorPlan`,

## What arrives formatted, and what the parts compute

Labels, dates, units, `display` values and deltas are pre-formatted strings. Three
parts must do arithmetic, so they take NUMBERS plus a `format` function (the app owns
currency and locale; the default is `€385,000`):

| part | computes |
| --- | --- |
| `PriceHistoryChart` | the Y domain and ticks, the path, the headline and the chart summary |
| `PriceEstimate` | the bar positions, the verdict and its percentage |
| `PricePerAreaComparison` | bar lengths (`value / largest value`); the label is `display` |
| `NeighbourhoodScores` | `value / max`; the label is `display`, default `value` to one decimal |

## `EnergyLabel` and `EnergyBadge`

Seven bars, A → G, 24 tall and 4 apart; A is 40% of the scale column and each class
adds 10%. The colours are Bloom's own scale on the theme: a fixed lightness curve
(dark green, up to a light yellow at D, down to a deep red) with the HUES taken from
the theme's `success` (A), `warning` (E) and `error` (G), interpolated in OKLCH. The
letter is white or neutral-950, whichever contrasts more — at least 4.5:1 in every
preset and mode (gated).

Each rating given (`consumption`, `emissions`) gets a column with an arrow tag on its
class's row, in the class colour: "C · 112 kWh/m²·year". With both columns below 560
wide the tags hold the letter only and the values move under the scale, so nothing
truncates on a phone. `pending` draws the scale neutral, no tags, and "Certificate in
progress" (`pendingLabel`).

The label is one image with a composed name ("Energy rating. Consumption: C,
112 kWh/m²·year. Emissions: D, 24 kg CO₂/m²·year.").

`EnergyBadge` is the class inline — a neutral pill (20 / 24 tall, `size`) with a disc in
the class colour and `label` ("Energy"; `""` for the class alone). Without `rating`, or
with `pending`, it draws a clock and "Pending". Named "Energy rating C".

## `PriceHistoryChart`

Built on the chart-card primitives (`ChartCardSurface`, `PeriodChartHeader`,
`CartesianPlot`): the headline is the current price and follows the hovered point;
`periods` are the 1Y / 3Y / All segments, each with its own `data` and `events`.

- `shape="step"` (default) holds each price until it changes — an asking price does not
  glide between listings. `shape="line"` joins the points.
- The Y axis is nice ticks AROUND the prices, not from zero, so a 5% drop is visible.
- `events` are markers on their point, coloured by `kind`: `listed` primary,
  `price-drop` success, `price-rise` warning, `rented` / `sold` text, `delisted`
  tertiary — and repeated as text in a legend under the plot.
- The last point carries a "Now · €385,000" callout (`currentLabel`), hidden while a
  point is hovered.
- Fewer than two points draw "No price history yet" (`emptyLabel`) instead of a plot.

**Accessible summary.** The plot's `role="img"` is named by a composed summary: "Price
history, 1Y: from €405,000 in September 2025 to €385,000 in March 2026. Listed,
September 2025. Price drop −5%, March 2026." (`accessibilityLabel` overrides it). Give
points a `title` so the summary reads in full months, not axis labels.

## `PriceEstimate`

An estimate that explains itself and never implies precision it does not have.

- The range as a heading, then a bar: the band `low` → `high`, the point `estimate` as
  a tick, the `asking` price as a taller marker with its label, and the ends labelled.
- The verdict compares `asking` with the NEAREST edge of the range: inside is "Fair
  price" (success); below is "Below estimate by 6%" (success); above is "Above estimate
  by 8%" — warning up to `highAboveRatio` (10%), error beyond. `formatVerdict`
  translates it.
- `confidence` is a 3-segment meter (a `progressbar`, 1–3) with its label and
  `confidenceNote`.
- "Why this estimate" expands `reasons` and the `comparables` count; the footer joins
  `method`, `version` and `updated`.

### Confidence changes the drawing, not just the label

| confidence | band drawn | core band | estimate tick | verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `high` | `low` → `high` | accent | shown | shown |
| `medium` | widened by 25% of the range each side, soft | accent | shown | shown |
| `low` | widened by 50% of the range each side, soft | **soft, no firm edge** | **hidden** | **replaced by "Not enough data for a verdict"** |

`confidence: 'low'` widens the visual band and hides the verdict. The numbers under
the band stay the model's range — only the picture refuses to look precise.

## `PricePerAreaComparison`

Rows of label + `display` over an 8-tall bar from zero; the `highlight` row is primary,
the rest neutral. A named list; each row reads "This home: €2,790/m²".

## `NeighbourhoodScores` and `NearbyPlaces`

`NeighbourhoodScores` draws each score as a bar (`variant="bars"`, default) or a 56px
ring (`rings`), with an optional icon and a short `description`. The labels are yours
("Transport", "Schools", "Quiet"…); `max` defaults to 10. Columns: 1 below 560 wide,
2 from 560 (`columns`). Every bar or ring is a `progressbar` named by its label with
flat `aria-valuemin/max/now` and `aria-valuetext` ("9.2 out of 10").

`NearbyPlaces` rows: a 40 icon disc, name and category, and the walking time on the
right (`modeIcon` for another mode). Each row reads "Lapa station, Metro, 4 min walk"
(`formatTime`).

## `RentHistoryList`

The rents (or prices) a place has had, for a place page that outlives its listings:
amount, period, note, and a small `Chip` for the delta. A leading "+" is warning, "−"
success (`deltaTone` overrides). An empty list draws "No history for this home yet".

## Accessibility

- Charts and scales are images with composed names; the history legend repeats the
  events as text.
- Every meter is a `progressbar` with flat `aria-value*`.
- "Why this estimate" carries `aria-expanded` (web) and `expanded` (native).
- Nothing relies on colour alone: verdicts are words, events are labelled, the energy
  class is a letter.
