## Evidence and history

Before local installs or tests, follow [verification](verification.mdx). It records native-resolution gaps, crash baselines, mirror hazards, and the required suite-union checks. For a specific measured regression, use the linked audit as evidence and retain its identifying environment and artifact hashes; do not generalize a fixture result beyond its scope.
